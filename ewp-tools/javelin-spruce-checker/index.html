Inspect or correct Javelin material lines (session only)

Corrections affect this browser comparison only. Review the original PDF before changing a line. Strikeouts are ignored automatically. To exclude a crossed-out row, select Exclude on that row and Apply; no changes are saved to the PDFs or database.

A match requires exact cuts and quantities where comparable. Stock pieces replacing shorter cuts are not automatically approved. Highlights are ignored and never create review flags. Crossed-out materials must be manually excluded when appropriate; unsupported stock substitutions may need checking. Flattened/handwritten marks and unreadable PDF pages may not be detectable. This is a checking aid, not a substitute for ordering review.
